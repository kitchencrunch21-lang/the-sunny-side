Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

The short version

The Sunny Side collects as little as possible. There are no accounts, no tracking profiles, and nothing is sold to anyone, ever.

What is collected

  • Newsletter signups: if you subscribe to The Sunday Glow, your email address is stored so the email can be sent. You can unsubscribe anytime with one click.
  • Contact form: messages sent through the contact form include whatever you type, plus your name and email so a reply is possible.
  • Basic analytics: anonymous, aggregated visit counts may be collected to see which articles readers enjoy. Nothing that identifies you personally.

Cookies

This site may use a small number of cookies for basic functionality and anonymous analytics. You can block cookies in your browser settings; the site will still work.

Third parties

Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which may receive your IP address as part of serving the font files. Email delivery, if you subscribe, is handled by a standard email provider.

Your rights

You can ask for a copy of any personal data held about you, or ask for it to be deleted, by emailing hello@thesunnyside.blog.

Changes

If this policy changes in a meaningful way, the update date at the top will change and a note will appear on the homepage for 30 days.
